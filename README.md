I'm Mohammed Mudassir Uddin, a researcher and builder from Hyderabad. I finished my B.E. in Computer Science at MJCET, Osmania University, in 2026. I came in through the diploma route and have been writing code for paying clients since I was 17.

Most of my work comes back to three questions. What is a model actually computing? What still works once it has to run on cheap hardware? And does a system behave the way its own explanation says it does?

**Research:** mechanistic interpretability · long-context architectures · LLM efficiency · edge ML · explanation faithfulness

### Papers

**[Layer Order and Key Binding Decide When Hybrid Models Learn to Retrieve](https://mudassiruddin7.github.io/Attention-Sink/)**<br/>
<sub>First author · under review at ICLR 2027 · [project page](https://mudassiruddin7.github.io/Attention-Sink/)</sub><br/>
49 models, 15 designs, one mechanism changed at a time. With the global layer first and keys bound, all 11 runs learned retrieval by step 300 and kept 98% recall at 64× the training length.

**[Hierarchical Sparse Circuit Extraction from Billion-Parameter Language Models through Scalable Attribution Graph Decomposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12879)**<br/>
<sub>First author · arXiv 2601.12879 · under review at Discover Artificial Intelligence · [project page](https://mudassiruddin7.github.io/hagd/) · [code](https://github.com/Mudassiruddin7/HAGD)</sub><br/>
Coarse-to-fine circuit search, 0.09 s over 413 features. Released with a five-seed verification suite and a public audit of which claims the code reproduces.

**[Meta-Learning Guided Pruning for Few-Shot Plant Pathology on Edge Devices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02353)**<br/>
<sub>First author · arXiv 2601.02353 · under review at JAIR · [project page](https://mudassiruddin7.github.io/pmp-dacis/) · [code](https://github.com/Mudassiruddin7/PMP-DACIS)</sub><br/>
ResNet-18 cut from 11.2M to 2.5M parameters. 142 ms per image on a Raspberry Pi 4, and 3.8 points above Meta-Prune at the same size.

**[Small Object Detection in Drone Aerial Imagery with LAF-YOLOv10](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14560)**<br/>
<sub>Co-author · arXiv 2609.14560 · [project page](https://mudassiruddin7.github.io/laf-yolov10/) · [code](https://github.com/Mudassiruddin7/Small-Object-Detection-in-UAV-Imagery)</sub><br/>
Four modules that each helped on their own lost 7.8 points of mAP when combined. We traced the loss to one interaction.

**[AgentCompress: Task-Aware Compression for Affordable Large Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05191)**<br/>
<sub>Co-author · arXiv 2601.05191 · [project page](https://mudassiruddin7.github.io/agentcompress/) · [code](https://github.com/Mudassiruddin7/AgentCompress)</sub><br/>
Routes each agent step to FP16, INT8, INT4 or INT2. The paper reports 68.3% less compute at 96.2% task success over 290 research workflows.

### Things I've built

- **[LaTeX Intelligence](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=MudassirUddinMohammed.latex-intelligence):** a VS Code extension that turns LaTeX into a Word file you can actually edit. Equations become native Word equations and cross-references stay live. 397 tests, 153 KB, no runtime dependencies. [Source](https://github.com/Mudassiruddin7/LaTex-Intelligence).
- **[Federated Data Node](https://github.com/Mudassiruddin7/Federated-Learning):** collects Telugu, Tamil, Hindi and Bengali text without the raw text ever leaving the device. Four independent egress guards, and it runs on a 4 GB Raspberry Pi over 2G.
- **[Guardian-AI](https://github.com/Mudassiruddin7/Guardian-AI):** a prompt-injection filter for LLMs that read security logs. It re-runs 34 rules on decoded and homograph-folded input, and caught 12 of 12 obfuscated attacks on a regression set at 1.1 ms median.
- **[AlphaEarth](https://github.com/Mudassiruddin7/AlphaEarth):** flood and wildfire damage for each land parcel from two Sentinel-2 passes. 24,608 parcels, leave-one-event-out validation, flood macro-F1 of 0.906.
- **Open source:** [11 pull requests to pgmpy](https://github.com/pgmpy/pgmpy/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3AMudassiruddin7) and [4 to pyGAM](https://github.com/dswah/pyGAM/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3AMudassiruddin7), mostly correctness fixes: d-separation, inference, and p-values via Wood's rank-r eigendecomposition.

**Also:** Polaris Fellow 2026, one of ten selected nationally · Erdős Institute Research Cohort 2026 · built at Agentathon 2025, the 2,300-developer hackathon that set a Guinness World Record.
